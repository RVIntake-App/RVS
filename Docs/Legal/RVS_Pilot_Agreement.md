## Pilot Agreement

This Pilot Agreement ("**Agreement**") is between **Arnold Digital Solutions LLC**, doing business as **RV Intake** ("**RV Intake**", "**we**"), and **[Shop / Business Legal Name]** ("**Customer**", "**you**"), doing business as **[DBA, if different]**, for the location(s) at **[Address]** (each, a "**Location**").

### 1. What you get

RV Intake provides a hosted intake form, AI-assisted diagnostic questions, and an emailed service packet (readable write-up, photos, printable PDF) for each Location, plus a customer status link. Full description: `rvintake.com` and the current product overview.

### 2. Pilot term and price

- The pilot runs **30 days from the date below**, free, starting the day the first Location's intake slug goes live.
- After 30 days, billing begins automatically at the rate checked below unless either party cancels first — no separate signature required to convert.

| Plan | Rate | Applies when |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ Mobile technician | **$39/mo**, locked for the life of this Agreement | No fixed shop location |
| ☐ Shop, 1–2 locations | **$79/location/mo** | Independent shop |
| ☐ Shop, 3–9 locations | **$69/location/mo** | Small group |
| ☐ Shop, 10+ locations | **$59/location/mo** | Larger group |

Two months free on annual prepay. Fair use: 300 submissions/location/month; extraordinary volume is a conversation, not an automatic overage charge. Price is locked for this Customer at the rate checked above for as long as the Agreement continues, regardless of future list-price changes.

### 3. Cancellation

Either party may cancel with written notice (email is sufficient) effective at the end of the current billing month. No minimum term beyond the free pilot period. No cancellation fee.

### 4. Anonymized data license (X-3)

Customer grants **Arnold Digital Solutions LLC and its successors and assigns** a **perpetual, irrevocable license** to use data submitted through the Location's intake form — service requests, diagnostic categories, and outcomes — in **anonymized, aggregated form**, stripped of any information identifying Customer, its Location(s), its customers, or any individual. RV Intake will never sell or share Customer's or its customers' identifiable information. This license survives cancellation of this Agreement and any assignment under §7.

### 5. Confidentiality and data ownership

Customer retains ownership of its raw service data. RV Intake will not disclose Customer's identifiable data to any third party except as needed to operate the service (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery) or as required by law.

### 6. No warranty; limitation of liability

The service is provided "as is" during the pilot and thereafter. RV Intake's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the three months preceding any claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

### 7. Assignment

We may assign this Agreement, without Customer's consent, to an affiliate or to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of all or substantially all of the RV Intake business or assets. We will tell Customer in writing (email is sufficient) when we do. Customer may not assign this Agreement without our written consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold. This Agreement binds and benefits each party's permitted successors and assigns.

### 8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah.
